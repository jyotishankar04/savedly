Share a single memory or a whole collection, with as much or as little control as you want.

<GuideActions actions={[{ label: "Shared with me", href: "/app/shared" }]} />
![Sharing screen](/help/docs/share.png)

## Turn on a link

From a memory or collection's share menu, choose a mode: public, password-protected, or invite-only with access requests.

![A memory's menu, with Share](/help/docs/share-1.png)

![The share dialog with the access options](/help/docs/share-2.png)

## Invite someone directly

Add their email. If they don't have an account yet, access is granted automatically the moment they sign up with that address.

## Manage access anytime

Revoke one person's access, or turn the link off entirely, from the same share panel — nothing is permanent.

![The Shared page, listing what you've shared](/help/docs/share-3.png)
