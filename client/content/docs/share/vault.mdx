A PIN-gated space for memories you'd rather keep out of your regular views.

<GuideActions actions={[{ label: "Open Vault", href: "/app/vault" }]} />
![Vault screen](/help/docs/vault.png)

## Set a PIN

The first time you open the Vault, you'll be asked to set a PIN for it.

![The vault's PIN setup screen](/help/docs/vault-1.png)

## Move something in

From any memory or collection's menu, choose "Move to Vault" — it disappears from your normal views immediately.

![A memory's menu, with Move to vault](/help/docs/vault-2.png)

## Unlock to view

Enter your PIN to see Vault contents; it locks again automatically the moment the tab loses focus. Worth knowing: this is PIN-gated access control, not end-to-end encryption of the content itself.

![The locked vault, asking for the PIN](/help/docs/vault-3.png)
