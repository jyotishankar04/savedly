## Keyword search

Works immediately for every account, no setup — matches the actual words in what you saved.

![Search results for a word that appears in the memories](/help/docs/search-1.png)

## Semantic search

Matches by meaning, not just words — "that pricing page" can find it even without the word "pricing" anywhere on it. This is powered by embeddings, which SaveForLatter covers by default at no cost to you, so it works out of the box.

![Search results for a phrase that means the same thing](/help/docs/search-2.png)

## Filter your results

Narrow by type, collection, tag, or a date range from the search page's filter bar.

![The search page with the collection filter open](/help/docs/search-3.png)
