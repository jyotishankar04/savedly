---

Everything below works anywhere in the dashboard. Use Cmd instead of Ctrl on Mac.
![Keyboard shortcuts screen](/help/docs/shortcuts.png)

## Search and capture

Ctrl+K opens the search palette. Ctrl+Q opens Quick Capture, and Ctrl+Enter submits it.

![The search palette, opened with Ctrl+K](/help/docs/shortcuts-1.png)

## Move around

Ctrl+B collapses or expands the sidebar. Ctrl+M opens the quick-nav menu when the sidebar is collapsed.

![The sidebar, collapsed with Ctrl+B](/help/docs/shortcuts-2.png)

![The quick-nav menu, opened with Ctrl+M](/help/docs/shortcuts-3.png)

## Browser-reserved keys

Ctrl+N (Notifications) and Ctrl+P (Profile) are also bound, but most browsers claim them first for new window and print — use the sidebar instead there.
