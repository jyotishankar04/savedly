## Update your profile & appearance

Settings → Account for your profile, Settings → Appearance for theme and accent color.

![The Account settings page](/help/docs/account-1.png)

## Export or delete your data

Settings → Privacy & Data → export everything you've saved as JSON, or as an Open Knowledge Format (OKF) folder of Markdown files that any AI agent can read. You can also permanently delete your account there.

![The Privacy & data settings page](/help/docs/account-2.png)

## Check your plan and usage

Settings → Plan & usage shows your plan, how much of each limit you've used, and how much included AI is left this month. Lite and Pro add more room, more AI and features like the private vault and bulk actions. To upgrade, pick a plan there; to change or cancel a paid plan, click Manage billing. Adding your own AI key in Settings → AI removes the AI limits.

![The Billing settings page](/help/docs/account-3.png)
