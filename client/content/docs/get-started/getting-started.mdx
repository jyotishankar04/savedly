## Create your account

Sign up with Google or GitHub — no password to remember, and no card required at any point.

![The sign-up page, with Google and GitHub sign-in](/help/docs/getting-started-1.png)

![The first onboarding step, asking for your name](/help/docs/getting-started-1-onboard.png)

## Connect an AI key

On the hosted plans, AI is included, so there's nothing to add: summaries, tags and Ask work straight away. On a self-hosted install, head to Settings → AI and add a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Google, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Saving, keyword search, and organizing manually work without a key.

![Settings, AI section, showing that the plan includes AI](/help/docs/getting-started-2.png)

## Save your first memory

Paste a link, drop a file, or just type a note from the capture bar on your dashboard.

![The dashboard with the capture bar and quick actions](/help/docs/getting-started-3.png)
