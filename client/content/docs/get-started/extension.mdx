Save the page you're on without leaving it. The Savedly extension for Chrome saves pages, links, selected text, images and screenshots straight into your library, and uses the sign-in you already have at savedly.app.

## Install it

1. Open Savedly in the Chrome Web Store and choose **Add to Chrome**.
2. Pin it: open Chrome's extensions menu (the puzzle piece) and pin Savedly, so its icon stays in the toolbar.
3. Sign in at savedly.app in the same browser. The extension uses that sign-in, so there's nothing to set up inside it.

## Save the page you're on

Click the Savedly icon in the toolbar. It shows the page's title and address, and you can add:

- **A note**, about why you're saving it. It helps you find it later.
- **Tags**, by typing one and pressing Enter.
- **A collection** to put it in.

Then choose **Save to Savedly**. As with anything you save, Savedly reads it, writes a short summary and suggests tags.

## Right-click to save

Right-click anywhere on a page and choose **Save to Savedly** to save:

- the page itself,
- the **text you've selected**,
- a **link**, without opening it,
- an **image**.

## Take a screenshot

From the toolbar popup, choose **Region** to drag around part of the page, or **Visible** to capture what's on screen. The right-click menu has both under **Capture screenshot**: **Select region**, and **Full page**, which also captures what's on screen. The screenshot is saved as an image, and Savedly reads any text in it, so you can search for that text later.

## The shortcut

Press **Ctrl+Shift+S** (**Cmd+Shift+S** on a Mac) to save the current page at once, without opening anything. To change the keys, open `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.

## The button on the page

A small Savedly button sits in the corner of each page, with the same options: save the page, or take a screenshot of an area or of what's on screen.

![The Savedly button on a page, open to show Save this page and the two screenshot options](/help/docs/extension-page-button.png)

If you'd rather not see it, turn it off with the eye icon at the top of the extension's popup. The toolbar icon, right-click menu and shortcut keep working.

## Privacy

Nothing about a page is sent until you choose to save it, and the extension doesn't collect your browsing history. The [privacy policy](/privacy#browser-extension) has the details.
