## Connect a calendar

Settings → Integrations → connect Google Calendar.

![The Integrations page, with Google Calendar](/help/docs/calendar-1.png)

## Let AI catch events

With AI configured, a saved memory that reads like an appointment or deadline gets flagged, with a one-click option to push it to your connected calendar.

![The Notifications page, with an event AI found](/help/docs/calendar-2.png)

## Or just ask

Tell Ask Savedly directly — "add a meeting with Alex tomorrow at 3pm" — and it's created and synced without opening a separate form.

![The calendar week view, with the event](/help/docs/calendar-3.png)
