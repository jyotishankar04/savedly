![Ask SaveForLatter screen](/help/docs/ask.png)

## Connect a reasoning model

On the hosted plans Ask is already set up. On a self-hosted install, assign a key to the "Reasoning" role in Settings → AI; that's the one role Ask needs to produce a reply.

## Ask a question

Open the full Ask page, or use the floating button available from every page except Ask itself.

![Ask answering a question, with the memories it used](/help/docs/ask-2.png)

## Let it act, not just answer

Ask it to save a note, edit a memory's title or tags, file something into a collection, or delete something — it can carry these out directly now, not just search and report back.

![Ask saving a note from a request](/help/docs/ask-3.png)

## Popup or sidebar, your choice

The floating widget can float near the corner or dock as a full-height sidebar — toggle it from the widget's own header, and both position and size are remembered next time.

![The Ask popup open in the corner of the dashboard](/help/docs/ask-4.png)
