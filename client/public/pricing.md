# Pricing — SaveForLatter

SaveForLatter offers a hosted cloud version and a fully featured self-hosted community version.

## Self-Hosted (Community)
- Price: $0
- Limits: Unlimited saves, unlimited history
- AI Access: Bring your own AI keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Google, etc.)
- Features: Every feature unlocked, local or S3 storage, custom SMTP

## Free (Hosted)
- Price: $0/month
- Limits: Essential storage limits apply
- AI Access: Supplied by SaveForLatter
